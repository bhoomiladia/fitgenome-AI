# Macro-Balancing Strategies by Fitness Goal

## Understanding Macronutrient Ratios

Macronutrient ratios should be tailored to the individual's fitness goal, body composition, activity level, and metabolic profile. The following guidelines are based on sports nutrition research and practical coaching experience.

## Goal: Lose Weight (Fat Loss)

### Recommended Macro Split: 40P / 30C / 30F
- **Calorie Target**: TDEE minus 400-500 kcal (moderate deficit)
- **Protein**: 2.0-2.4g per kg bodyweight — preserves lean mass during deficit
- **Carbs**: Prioritize around workouts (pre/post), reduce at other meals
- **Fat**: Minimum 0.8g per kg bodyweight to maintain hormonal function

### Strategy
- High protein at every meal to maintain satiety and muscle
- Front-load carbs in the first half of the day and around training
- Increase vegetable volume for fiber and fullness (target 30g fiber/day)
- Weekly refeed day at maintenance calories (higher carbs) every 7-14 days
- Rate of loss: 0.5-1% bodyweight per week (sustainable)

### Indian-Specific Tips
- Replace rice with cauliflower rice or ragi roti to reduce calorie density
- Use paneer and dal as primary protein — avoid fried preparations (skip pakoras)
- Opt for tandoori/grilled over curry-based preparations
- Use 1 tsp ghee instead of 1 tbsp — small change, big calorie savings
- Cucumber raita instead of heavy cream-based sides

## Goal: Build Muscle (Hypertrophy)

### Recommended Macro Split: 30P / 45C / 25F
- **Calorie Target**: TDEE plus 250-400 kcal (lean bulk)
- **Protein**: 1.6-2.2g per kg bodyweight
- **Carbs**: 4-6g per kg bodyweight — fuel for intense training
- **Fat**: 0.8-1.2g per kg bodyweight

### Strategy
- Surplus should be moderate to minimize fat gain (lean bulk approach)
- Distribute protein evenly across 4-5 meals (30-40g per meal)
- High-carb meals around training window (pre + post workout)
- Emphasize complex carbs: oats, rice, sweet potato, whole wheat
- Track weekly weight gain: target 0.25-0.5% bodyweight per week

### Indian-Specific Tips
- Extra roti/rice portions at lunch and dinner
- Post-workout: banana shake with milk and peanut butter
- Add nuts and seeds to every meal for calorie density
- Dal with extra ghee provides both protein and calories
- Bedtime: warm milk with turmeric and almonds (casein-like slow protein)

## Goal: Maintain Weight

### Recommended Macro Split: 30P / 40C / 30F
- **Calorie Target**: TDEE (no surplus or deficit)
- **Protein**: 1.6-2.0g per kg bodyweight
- **Carbs**: 3-5g per kg bodyweight
- **Fat**: 1.0-1.3g per kg bodyweight

### Strategy
- Balanced approach with no extreme restrictions
- Focus on food quality and micronutrient density
- Maintain consistent meal timing
- Allow 10-20% flexible eating (social meals, treats)
- Monitor weight weekly — adjust by 100-200 kcal if drifting

## Goal: Improve Endurance

### Recommended Macro Split: 25P / 50C / 25F
- **Calorie Target**: TDEE plus 100-200 kcal
- **Protein**: 1.4-1.8g per kg bodyweight
- **Carbs**: 5-8g per kg bodyweight — primary fuel for endurance
- **Fat**: 0.8-1.0g per kg bodyweight

### Strategy
- Carbohydrate periodization: higher on training days, moderate on rest days
- Pre-endurance session: easily digestible carbs (banana, toast, poha)
- During long sessions (>60 min): 30-60g carbs per hour (dates, glucose)
- Post-session: rapid glycogen replenishment with high-GI carbs + protein
- Hydration with electrolytes is critical

### Indian-Specific Tips
- Idli/dosa as pre-run meals — easily digestible
- Chikki (jaggery + peanut bar) during long runs
- Sattu drink (roasted gram flour) — traditional energy drink
- Dates and bananas during training

## Protein Timing Research
- Distribute protein across 4-6 meals for optimal muscle protein synthesis (MPS)
- Each meal should contain at least 20-30g of high-quality protein
- Post-workout protein window: within 2 hours (not as narrow as once believed)
- Pre-sleep protein (casein or curd-based): 30-40g supports overnight MPS
- Leucine threshold: 2.5-3g per meal triggers maximal MPS (found in eggs, dairy, chicken)
