# Indian Nutrition Guide for Fitness

## Complex Carbohydrate Sources
- **Whole wheat roti**: 70 kcal per roti (30g), 2.5g protein, 15g carbs
- **Brown rice**: 112 kcal per 100g cooked, 2.6g protein, 24g carbs
- **Oats**: 389 kcal per 100g raw, 17g protein, 66g carbs
- **Jowar (Sorghum) roti**: Gluten-free, high fiber, 72 kcal per roti
- **Bajra (Pearl Millet)**: Iron-rich, 361 kcal per 100g raw
- **Ragi (Finger Millet)**: Calcium-rich, 328 kcal per 100g
- **Sweet potato**: 86 kcal per 100g, excellent pre-workout carb
- **Poha (Flattened rice)**: Quick-digesting, iron-fortified

## Healthy Fat Sources
- **Ghee**: 1 tsp = 45 kcal, improves nutrient absorption, use 1-2 tsp/meal
- **Mustard oil**: Rich in MUFA, traditional cooking oil
- **Coconut**: MCTs for quick energy, 354 kcal per 100g
- **Almonds**: 576 kcal per 100g, 21g protein
- **Walnuts**: 654 kcal per 100g, omega-3 rich
- **Flaxseeds (Alsi)**: 534 kcal per 100g, omega-3, add to smoothies
- **Peanut butter**: 2 tbsp = 190 kcal, 7g protein

## Indian Meal Timing Framework

### Pre-Workout (30-60 min before)
- 1 banana + 1 tbsp peanut butter
- Poha with peanuts
- 2 toast with honey

### Post-Workout (within 30 min)
- Whey protein shake + banana
- Paneer bhurji + 2 rotis
- Egg white omelette + rice

### Meal Structure Template
| Meal | Time | Components |
|------|------|-----------|
| Breakfast | 7:00-8:00 AM | Protein + Complex Carb + Fruit |
| Mid-Morning | 10:30 AM | Nuts/Seeds + Buttermilk |
| Lunch | 12:30-1:30 PM | Dal/Sabzi + Roti/Rice + Salad + Curd |
| Evening Snack | 4:00-5:00 PM | Sprouts chaat / Roasted chana |
| Dinner | 7:30-8:30 PM | Lighter protein + Roti + Vegetable |
| Before Bed | 10:00 PM | Warm turmeric milk (haldi doodh) |

## Regional Cuisine Options

### North Indian
- Chole (chickpea curry) + whole wheat roti
- Rajma chawal (kidney bean curry + rice)
- Dal makhani + tandoori roti
- Palak paneer + jeera rice

### South Indian
- Idli + sambar + coconut chutney (excellent pre-workout)
- Dosa + dal filling (protein dosa)
- Ragi mudde + saaru (Karnataka)
- Pesarattu (green gram dosa, Andhra Pradesh)

### Bengali
- Machher jhol (fish curry) + rice
- Cholar dal (Bengal gram) + luchi
- Shukto (mixed vegetable) — high fiber

### Gujarati
- Dhokla (fermented, light, probiotic)
- Undhiyu (mixed vegetable, fiber-rich)
- Handvo (savory cake, protein from besan)

## Hydration Guidelines
- Minimum 3-4 liters water daily
- Buttermilk (chaas): electrolytes + probiotics
- Coconut water: natural electrolyte replacement
- Nimbu pani (lemon water): vitamin C + hydration
- Avoid excessive chai/coffee (limit to 2-3 cups)
